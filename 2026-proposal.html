Working Partnership

A platform, a partnership,
and a stake in what it becomes.

EchoTribe will build and run Mommy & Me Collective's shopping-collections platform and operating tools, source and manage new brand deals, and run paid media — paired with a base retainer, performance upside, and founder-client participation in EchoTribe itself.

I

Scope of services

Shopping platform

The shopping-collections storefront on your owned domain, plus the internal admin tools that run it — a fast "drop a deal" curator flow for Steph and a prioritized "post this next" worklist for Lainey.

Brand deals & sponsorships

Sourcing and managing new affiliate partnerships and paid sponsorships — building toward a reliable monthly brand-deal pipeline.

Paid Meta advertising

Building and running paid campaigns on Meta, funded by Mommy & Me Collective, to amplify the products and collections that are already converting.

Attribution & insights

The reporting layer that finally answers where the revenue comes from — by channel, by collection, and by what your audience actually buys.

III

Platform license & ownership

  • EchoTribe owns the platform — source code, recommendation engine, attribution layer, and data models remain the property of EchoTribe Holdings, LLC.
  • Mommy & Me Collective receives a license to use the platform for its business throughout the term of this partnership.
  • You keep your audience. Mommy & Me Collective retains its data, customer and audience relationships; EchoTribe retains platform and data models.
Founder-Client Privilege

A stake in the upside you helped build.

10%

As EchoTribe's founding design partner, Mommy & Me Collective receives a participation right equal to 10% of net proceeds if EchoTribe is acquired — recognition that this partnership shaped the platform, and a share in its long-term success.

The right is contingent on an active partnership and is defined in full in the definitive agreement.

IV

Term & approvals

Term
Initial term of three months, then continues month to month.
Notice
Either party may end the partnership with 30 days' written notice.
Approvals
Brand sponsorhips and financial commitments are approved by Mommy & Me Collective.
Definitive terms
License, data rights, and acquisition participation to be set in a definitive agreement prepared with counsel.

If this structure works for you both, the next step is a definitive agreement reflecting these terms. This proposal is a starting point for that conversation — numbers and terms can be adjusted together. June 1st, 2026 is the requested retoactive date to begin the partnership.
